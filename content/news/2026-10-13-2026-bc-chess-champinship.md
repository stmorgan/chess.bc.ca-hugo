Congratulations to our new BC Champion, NM Yidong (Jason) Chen.  Yidong won on tie-break over FM Jingyun (Ryan) Yang, CM Zachary Liu and FM Grigorii Morozov, all with 4.5/7.

The BC Women's Championship was won by Dahlia Quinn with 5/6.  Charlotte Prokscha finished 2nd with 4.5/6 and Sofia Vigurs 3rd with 4/6.
