The contestants for the BC Championship have just been announced.  The event will take place at the Comfort Inn and Suites on Fraser Highway in Surrey over Thanksgiving Weekend 9-12 October.  The BC Women's Championship and an Open event will share the space.  Registration for the BC Women's Championship has not yet been finalized.  Details are on the Fraser Valley Chess website.
