---
title: How to Donate
---
The BCCF Foundation exists to provide long-term support for the future of chess in BC.

Please help chess thrive in BC though a one-time donation or legacy estate planning via the BCCF Foundation.  Your help today can support BC chess for decades.  To make a donation, please contact the Chairman of the BCCF Foundation, currently Paul Leblanc ([pc-leblanc@shaw.ca](mailto:pc-leblanc@shaw.ca)) or the BCCF Treasurer, currently Murray Chu ([murraychu@gmail.com](mailto:murraychu@gmail.com)).
